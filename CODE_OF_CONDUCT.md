# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Summary

- Be respectful. Disagreement is fine; harassment is not.
- Assume good faith. Ask before assuming malice.
- Vietnamese and English are both welcome in issues and PRs.
- No spam, no self-promotion outside the `Show & Tell` discussion category.

## Reporting

Report violations to the maintainer at github.com/saucevn (open a private issue or DM). Reports are confidential.
